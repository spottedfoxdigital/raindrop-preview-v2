# Raindrop Cleaning: Brand Direction (preview)

## Palette
One palette, built from Raindrop's orange and green. The orange and green are estimates (the logo is black and sky blue); confirm with James and Renee before launch.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (CTA) | Pumpkin | #B85409 | Primary buttons, phone links. White text on it: 4.9:1 (AA). |
| Primary hover | Pumpkin Dark | #9A4508 | Hover and focus state of primary buttons. |
| Secondary (accents) | Forest | #1F5E2E | Headings accents, icons, secondary button outline, footer background. White on it: 7.8:1. |
| Secondary light | Leaf | #2E7D32 | Small accents only (icon strokes, rules). Not for body text on tints. |
| Background tint | Mist | #EEF4EF | Alternating section background. |
| Background | Cream | #FBF8F3 | Page background. |
| Surface | White | #FFFFFF | Cards. |
| Text | Ink | #1C2420 | Body and headings. 15.0:1 on cream. |
| Muted text | Slate | #4F5A54 | Captions, secondary copy. 6.8:1 on cream. |
| Border | Sand | #E4DED3 | Card borders, dividers. |

Rule: orange means "call us" and nothing else. Green carries structure. No other hues.

## Type (Google Fonts)
- Headings: **Fraunces** (600), a warm, sturdy serif that feels family-run, not corporate.
- Body and UI: **Inter** (400, 600), highly legible on phones.
- Scale (mobile to desktop): H1 2.25rem to 3.25rem, H2 1.75rem to 2.25rem, body 1.0625rem, line height 1.6.

## Photo style
Real photos of James and Renee on real jobs only. Bright natural daylight, people at work, carpet texture visible. No stock. Crop tight on the work; one wide shot of the two of them with the van builds trust. WebP, under 200 KB, with descriptive alt text.

## Buttons
- **Primary (one style, used everywhere):** Pumpkin fill, white Inter 600 text, 8px radius, 14px by 28px padding, phone icon, label "Call 509-833-8257". Hover: Pumpkin Dark. Visible 3px Forest focus ring.
- **Secondary:** transparent with a 2px Forest border and Forest text, same shape. Used for low-priority, non-call actions (for example "See services").

## Tone of voice
Plain, warm, and local. Short sentences. Speak as a family business talking to a neighbor: "we" and "you". State facts, never hype; no invented claims, numbers, or reviews. No em dashes.
