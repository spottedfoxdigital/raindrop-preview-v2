# Raindrop Cleaning: Brand Direction (preview)

## Palette
One palette: Raindrop green plus warm neutrals. Orange is dropped for a cleaner, single-hue look (the earlier orange and green were estimates; the logo is black and sky blue). Confirm the green with James and Renee before launch.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary (CTA) | Forest | #1F5E2E | Primary buttons, mobile sticky call bar, phone links, eyebrows, step numbers, footer. White on it: 7.78:1 (AAA). On cream: 7.35:1. |
| Primary hover | Forest Dark | #174A24 | Hover state of primary buttons and phone links. White on it: 10.29:1. On cream: 9.71:1. |
| Accent | Leaf | #2E7D32 | Small decorative accents only (icon strokes, rules). Not for text. |
| Background tint | Mist | #EEF4EF | Alternating section background. Forest on it: 6.97:1. |
| Background | Cream | #FBF8F3 | Page background. |
| Surface | White | #FFFFFF | Cards, header. |
| Text | Ink | #1C2420 | Body, headings, secondary button. 14.99:1 on cream, 15.88:1 on white. |
| Muted text | Slate | #4F5A54 | Captions, secondary copy. 6.78:1 on cream. |
| Border | Sand | #E4DED3 | Card borders, dividers. |

Rule: Forest fill means "call us". Ink and neutrals carry everything else. No other hues.

## Type (Google Fonts)
- Headings: **Fraunces** (600), a warm, sturdy serif that feels family-run, not corporate.
- Body and UI: **Inter** (400, 600), highly legible on phones.
- Scale (mobile to desktop): H1 2.25rem to 3.25rem, H2 1.75rem to 2.25rem, body 1.0625rem, line height 1.6.

## Photo style
Real photos of James and Renee on real jobs only. Bright natural daylight, people at work, carpet texture visible. No stock. Crop tight on the work; one wide shot of the two of them with the van builds trust. WebP, under 200 KB, with descriptive alt text.

## Buttons
- **Primary (one style, used everywhere, including the mobile sticky call bar):** Forest #1F5E2E fill, white Inter 600 text (7.78:1), 8px radius, 16px by 28px padding, phone icon, label "Call 509-833-8257". Hover: Forest Dark #174A24 (10.29:1). Focus: 3px Ink outline.
- **Secondary:** white fill, 2px Ink #1C2420 border and Ink text (15.88:1), same shape. Neutral, so it never competes with or blends into the green primary. Used for low-priority, non-call actions (for example "See services").
- Phone links in text and the header: Forest, weight 600, Forest Dark with underline on hover.

## Logo
Header logo at 270px wide on mobile and 320px on desktop, on a white header bar.

## Tone of voice
Plain, warm, and local. Short sentences. Speak as a family business talking to a neighbor: "we" and "you". State facts, never hype; no invented claims, numbers, or reviews. No em dashes.
